Icon phase (0–1s) — the real exported app icon fades and springs in, holds just long enough to register as "the app icon," then scales up and dissolves while a handful of its own glyph characters burst outward (+0–0.6s from the dissolve).
Assembly phase (starts on that same dissolve, staggered ~130ms apart) — each piece begins as a small rotated, blurred shard and drops in with a spring overshoot; a brief amber flash marks the landing. The last piece is down at 2.36s and the overlay is gone: the launch hands over the moment it has something to hand over.
Loading rain — the rain is not a cold-start beat. It plays over a band still loading its own content (the sessions list's first load), clocked by that wait rather than by the icon, and its script lives on below under loading rain (sessions) reference; press loading rain to run it over the settled screen. Those burst glyphs are still the seed the rain grows from, not two unrelated effects stitched together — the same alphabet, spent where the waiting actually is.
